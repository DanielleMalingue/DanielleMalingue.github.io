Addison

Executive-function toolkit built from the ground up for people with ADHD.

In Development Join waitlist

Addison is an executive-function toolkit purpose-built for people with ADHD. Where most productivity software punishes the way ADHD brains work, Addison was designed around it — from the first interaction to the last.

It surfaces the one most important next action, reduces decision fatigue through structured daily planning, and adapts to your focus state in real time. Mobile-first. Accessible by design. Calm by default.
