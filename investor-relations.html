Investment thesis

Why this, why now.

Executive dysfunction costs the global workforce hundreds of billions annually in lost productivity. ADHD affects over 15 million US adults — yet virtually no software has been built with their cognition in mind.

Malingue Inc. takes a behavior-science-first approach. We do not layer AI on top of an existing paradigm. We design from the ground up around how people actually think, prioritize, and move — and build tools precise enough to matter.

Our two flagship products address adjacent moments in the same user journey: Godspeed for professionals who need execution speed; Addison for individuals who need structure and cognitive support. Together they anchor a platform thesis around human performance at work.

Market Global productivity software projected at $96B+ by 2025. Mental wellness apps growing at 16% CAGR.
TAM — ADHD 15M+ diagnosed adults in the US. Underserved by existing tools.
Differentiation Behavior-science foundation drives meaningfully higher engagement and retention.
Founder Full-stack capability: product design, brand, and engineering under one roof.
